Privacy & local data

Build 4.7.0

Current local mode

Provenance Studio currently stores the local profile, draft, and saved projects in browser storage on this device. The production identity-provider buttons remain disabled until a backend and provider credentials are configured.

Your controls

The Account panel lets you export your local Provenance data as JSON or delete the local profile, saved projects, backup, and current draft from this device.

Future account services

Apple, Google, Microsoft, or Facebook sign-in will not be represented as active until the selected production authentication service, redirect configuration, security controls, and provider credentials are implemented and tested. This page must be updated before those services are enabled.

Important device note

Removing browser or website data through the operating system can also remove locally stored Provenance data. Export important work before clearing browser data.
