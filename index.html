Study Assist
Canvas Lens

Runs inline over supported Canvas pages. It detects and deduplicates visible questions, ignores any current choice state, and keeps the page controls untouched. On active assessments it provides concise review hints; when Canvas itself exposes official post-submission feedback, that confirmed answer can be labeled. The Practice Autofill Lab demonstrates real radio, checkbox, dropdown, and text-field automation with one master ON/OFF control. In the practice sandbox, ON enables Auto-select, Auto-fill, and Auto-submit together; on live Canvas pages, the same control turns Lens assistance on or off.
